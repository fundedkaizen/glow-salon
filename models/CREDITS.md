# 3D model credits

Every model under `public/models/` is either our own work (built from the scripts in `assets-src/`) or built on
free assets released under **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain), which allows redistribution in this public repo.
No Mixamo, no paid packs and no NC licences are used. Each licence was checked on the pack's own page and in the
licence file inside the download (25 Sep 2026).

| What | Source | Licence | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Universal Base Characters [Standard] (free tier) by Quaternius | https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters and https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html | CC0 1.0 (page and `License_Standard.txt`) | The people's bodies, sculpted faces, eyes and brows (the Superhero female and male bodies, restyled: slimmer, shorter limbs, bigger head and eyes, muscles smoothed), and the long, buns, simple-parted and buzzed hair pieces with their strand textures |
| Universal Animation Library [Standard] (free tier) by Quaternius | https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library and https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html | CC0 1.0 (page and `License.txt`) | The rig's bone names and the walk cycle |

Our own work (CC0 like the rest of the repo's art): the outfits, shoes and accessories, the bob, ponytail, braids and
curly hair, every other animation clip (idle, talk, the four seated clips, work, wave, cheer, sleepy), and all the
furniture, stations, room parts, plants and decor, built by the Blender scripts in `assets-src/`.

Tools: Blender 5.2 (GPL; its output is ours), gltfpack 1.2 from meshoptimizer (MIT) for compression.
