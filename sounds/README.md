# Glow Salon sound kit

223 short clips (3.2 MB) cut from real recordings by Joseph Sardin at [BigSoundBank](https://bigsoundbank.com), released under **CC0** (public domain). No attribution is required, but credit is appreciated: "Additional sounds: Joseph SARDIN - BigSoundBank.com". `kit.json` lists every clip with its source page.

Each one-shot recording was split at its silences into separate hits, so every group has natural variations: play a random one, with slight pitch and volume variation, so repeats never sound the same. Loops (`*-loop-*`) are cross-faded to loop seamlessly. Everything is mono, 44.1 kHz, 96 kbps MP3, loudness-matched.
